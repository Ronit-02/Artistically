# Artistically — Project Overview

## Purpose

Artistically is an India-first, INR marketplace and discovery platform for curated original art. It helps independent creators present their work and helps collectors discover artwork with clear, art-specific information.

The product should feel like a dependable art marketplace rather than a generic store with art products added to it.

## Problem and promise

Artists often rely on disconnected social, messaging, gallery, and generic-commerce channels. Artistically addresses the resulting gaps in credible discovery, art-specific information, storefront operations, payment, and delivery.

For collectors, the promise is meaningful art from credible artists with clear artwork facts and dependable marketplace outcomes. For artists, it is a credible storefront and a connected path from listing to sale without assembling disconnected tools.

## Product direction

- Discovery and editorial merchandising lead the experience; dependable commerce remains the foundation.
- The initial collector is a first-time or emerging buyer who needs clear artwork facts, credible artists, understandable payment, and dependable delivery.
- Artistically owns editorial stories and editorial collections.
- Artists own their profiles, artwork, and artist-created collections containing only their own artwork.
- The public `Verified` label means Artistically reviewed the artist’s identity and background. It does not guarantee every artwork, price, availability claim, or future action.
- Reports create review cases rather than immediate enforcement. Validated cases may remove affected content, and affected owners may appeal.
- Application state is authoritative for identity, permissions, pricing, inventory, verification, orders, and moderation outcomes.

## Primary audiences

- Independent and established artists.
- First-time art buyers and experienced collectors.
- Curators, editors, designers, and gift buyers.
- Marketplace support and operations staff.

## Product areas

### Public marketplace

- Home, search, and category discovery.
- Artwork and artist pages.
- Collections and editorial stories.
- Informational, support, and policy pages.

### Collector experience

- Account and profile experiences.
- Wishlist, cart, and checkout.
- Orders, delivery tracking, cancellation, returns, refunds, and disputes.
- Reviews for eligible purchases.

### Artist experience

- Artist profile and onboarding.
- Artwork, media, inventory, and collection management.
- Orders, fulfillment, and delivery information.
- Sales, fees, balance, refunds, and payout information.

### Marketplace operations

- Account, artist, listing, review, and editorial administration.
- Verification, moderation, reports, appeals, disputes, and support workflows.
- Marketplace policies and accountable operational decisions.

## Product journey expectations

- A collector can discover an artwork, evaluate its maintained facts and artist context, purchase it through a supported checkout, follow the resulting order, and review an eligible delivered purchase.
- An artist can complete onboarding, maintain a profile and accurate listing, manage collections and fulfillment, and understand the disposition of sales, fees, refunds, and payouts.
- Marketplace staff can review verification, reports, appeals, disputes, and related decisions through accountable workflows rather than direct record edits.

## Initial-release scope

- A mobile-friendly web marketplace rather than native mobile applications.
- Artist onboarding, artwork listings, discovery, collections, cart, purchase, orders, fulfillment, reviews, trust operations, and essential administration.
- Physical and digital artwork with appropriate facts and delivery expectations.
- Policies and support information necessary for marketplace participation.

## Outside the initial release

- NFTs or cryptocurrency trading.
- Fractional ownership or investment products.
- Automated art valuation or investment-return predictions.
- Full gallery inventory or enterprise resource-planning functionality.
- Supporting every international tax, customs, and payout scenario at launch.
- Maintaining two application API styles simultaneously.

Social feeds, buyer-artist messaging, auctions, and optional room-preview capabilities are later possibilities and must not delay the trusted marketplace foundation.

## Product principles

- Artwork and factual listing information take priority over promotional decoration.
- Trust labels and marketplace claims must have a defined meaning.
- User journeys should work across mobile and desktop and remain accessible.
- New capabilities should build on established product and design patterns.

## Success signals

When measurement is approved through the privacy process, product decisions should consider artist-onboarding completion, time to first published listing and sale, listing-to-cart and checkout conversion, payment success, on-time delivery, refund and dispute rates, repeat buyer and seller activity, and eligible-review participation.

Internal workflows, security posture, service providers, confidential architecture, deployment assumptions, and operational readiness belong in the relevant ignored local files when present.
