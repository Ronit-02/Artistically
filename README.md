# 🎨 Artistically

A modern art e-commerce platform for discovering, exploring, and purchasing original artwork. Built with Next.js 14, TypeScript, Zustand, and Tailwind CSS to deliver a fast, scalable, and responsive shopping experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🖼️ Browse and explore curated artwork collections
- 🔎 Search and filter artworks by category, medium, and style
- ❤️ Save favorite artworks to your wishlist
- 🛒 Purchase artwork and track your orders
- 🎨 Sign up as a Creator to showcase and sell your artwork
- 🖌️ Sign up as a Collector to discover and purchase unique pieces
- 🏺 Explore a diverse range of artwork, including paintings, digital art, illustrations, photography, and sculptures
- 📖 Read inspiring stories, insights, and journeys from artists around the world
- 👤 Manage your profile, artworks, and order history

---

| Category | Technology |
|-----------|------------|
