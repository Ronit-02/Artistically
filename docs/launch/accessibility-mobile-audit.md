# Accessibility and mobile quality

Artistically aims to provide keyboard access, meaningful semantics, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, usable touch targets, and responsive reflow. WCAG 2.2 AA is the release bar.

## Required release checks

Verify keyboard paths, screen-reader labels and status, visible focus, contrast, motion controls, 44px touch targets, reflow at 320px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, and 1280px, and 200% zoom. Critical paths include marketplace navigation, search, product media, cart, account, artist workspace, dialogs, and operational tables.

Pay particular attention to carousel announcements, mobile scroller first/last items, form field-error mapping, and focus restoration from drawers and dialogs.

Device matrices, assistive-technology observations, defects, evidence, owners, and release sign-off are maintained in the approved internal quality record.
