# Legal and policy review

Review date: 2026-08-25  
This is an internal launch checklist, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel approval.

## Documents in the product

- `/privacy`: data collection, cookies/analytics position, retention, rights request route, vendors, and contact details.
- `/terms`: account use, marketplace role, listing rules, payment, order, dispute, limitation, and governing-law terms.
- `/shipping`: processing, shipment, tracking, damage, delay, digital-delivery, and support expectations.
- `/artist-guidelines`: listing accuracy, ownership, prohibited content, copyright, authenticity, and moderation rules.
- `/commissions`: commission calculation and seller settlement wording.
- `/help` and `/contact`: operational guidance and real support destinations.

## Official reference points for counsel

The legal owner should verify the launch position against the current [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/Digital%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf) and the [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%C3%AF%C2%BF%C2%BC), the [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection/consumer-protection), and the [CBIC e-commerce GST guidance](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/sectoral-faq.html). These references do not replace advice on the entity, product flow, tax treatment, or launch geography.
