# Legal and policy review

Public marketplace policies require review by the appropriate business and legal owners before release. This document is not legal advice.

The review covers consumer and seller terms; privacy and data rights; payment, refund, and dispute disclosures; content, copyright, authenticity, and takedown policy; digital-goods licensing; accessibility; marketing claims; tax treatment; and support escalation.

Public product pages must provide reviewed, page-specific privacy, terms, shipping, artist-guideline, commission, help, and contact content before launch.

Specific obligations, launch geography, tax treatment, policy gaps, counsel notes, approval status, and sign-off records are maintained in the approved internal legal record.
